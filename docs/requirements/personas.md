# Personas

> These personas are grounded in the assessment scenario (commuting under fuel
> quotas) and stated assumptions. Where you gather real input from classmates,
> record it and update these.

## Persona 1 — Amaya, the passenger-first student
- **Age / role:** 21, second-year student, does not own a car.
- **Context:** Lives in Maharagama, travels to campus 4 days a week. Relies on
  buses that are now more crowded and less predictable during the shortage.
- **Goals:** Find a reliable, affordable ride that fits her class times.
- **Frustrations:** Doesn't know who else lives nearby; ad-hoc WhatsApp groups
  are noisy and hard to search.
- **Needs from the app:** Search by route + time, request a seat, message the
  driver, see whether the ride is confirmed.

## Persona 2 — Kavindu, the driver with spare seats
- **Age / role:** 24, final-year student, drives a car with 3 free seats.
- **Context:** His plate lets him refuel on even days; he'd rather share costs
  and seats than drive empty.
- **Goals:** Fill seats with people on his route, keep control over who joins.
- **Frustrations:** Strangers messaging without context; no easy way to manage
  requests.
- **Needs from the app:** Post a ride, approve/decline requests, mark seats as
  taken automatically, message confirmed passengers.

## Persona 3 — Ms. Fernando, the staff commuter
- **Age / role:** 38, lecturer.
- **Context:** Fixed schedule, values privacy and predictability.
- **Goals:** A dependable shared commute with colleagues or trusted students.
- **Frustrations:** Concerned about sharing personal contact details publicly.
- **Needs from the app:** Share phone number only with confirmed ride partners,
  clear trip history, simple and professional interface.

## Assumptions
- Users have a smartphone or laptop with internet at least intermittently.
- Users are willing to coordinate final pickup details via in-app chat.
- Identity is tied to an ICBT-style email during registration.
