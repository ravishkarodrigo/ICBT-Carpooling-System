# Scenarios

Real-world use cases showing how personas use the system end to end.

## Scenario A — Amaya finds a morning ride
It's Sunday evening. Amaya has a 9:00 class tomorrow. She logs in, opens **Find a
ride**, and searches `from: Maharagama`, `to: ICBT Campus`, time window
`08:00–09:00`. The list shows Kavindu's ride first because both the route and
time window match. She opens it, sees 3 seats free, and taps **Request a seat**
with a short note. She gets a confirmation that the driver will review it.

## Scenario B — Kavindu manages requests
Monday morning Kavindu sees a notification: a new request on his ride. In **My
rides** he reads Amaya's note and taps **Accept**. The app automatically reduces
the seat count and, if that was the last seat, marks the ride **full**. Amaya is
notified she's confirmed, and a message thread opens so they can agree the exact
pickup point.

## Scenario C — Coordinating pickup in chat
Amaya messages "I'll wait by the Maharagama junction at 7:45". Kavindu replies in
near-real-time. Neither had to share a phone number publicly; the conversation is
scoped to this ride.

## Scenario D — A ride is cancelled
Kavindu's plate can't refuel that day, so he opens the ride and taps **Cancel
ride**. Its status becomes **cancelled**, it no longer appears in search, and it
moves to trip history for both of them.

## Scenario E — Ms. Fernando reviews history
At month end, Ms. Fernando opens **Trip history** to see the shared trips she
completed, useful for splitting costs with colleagues.
